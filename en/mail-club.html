Good to know

Shipping

Letters go out on the first of each month, to the address given when you signed up. Let me know if you move: a letter sent to your old address cannot be replaced.

Lost or damaged letter

If it reaches you damaged, write to me within 48 hours with photos and I will replace it. If it never arrives, let me know and we will sort it out with the post office.

Cancellation

You can stop whenever you like, without giving a reason, with a simple email. Cancellation applies from the next letter; a letter already prepared or shipped remains payable.

Refunds

Letters already shipped are not refunded. Any payment taken after your cancellation is refunded in full.
