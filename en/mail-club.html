Frequently asked questions

FAQ

When does my letter ship?

Letters ship on the first of each month, to the address given when you signed up. Let me know if you move: a letter sent to the old address cannot be replaced.

What if my letter arrives damaged or gets lost?

If it arrives damaged, write to me within 48 hours with photos: I will replace it. If it never arrives, let me know and we will sort it out with the post office.

How do I stop my subscription?

You can stop whenever you like, without giving a reason, by simple email. Cancellation applies to the next letter; a letter already prepared or shipped remains due.

Am I refunded?

Letters already shipped are not refunded. Any payment taken after your cancellation is refunded in full.
