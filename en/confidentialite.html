Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. English translation for information only; the French version prevails.

Dana Illustration cares about protecting your personal data, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data collected

When you place an order or get in touch, the following data may be collected: surname, first name, postal address, email address and the information needed for delivery. Payment data is processed directly by the payment provider (Shopify and its partners) and is not kept by the Seller.

Purposes

  • Processing and tracking orders;
  • Delivering products;
  • Answering contact requests;
  • Meeting legal and accounting obligations.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list for a product (for example the calendar), your name and email address are kept for the sole purpose of telling you when it goes on sale, then deleted once that message has been sent. You can ask for them to be removed at any time at dana.illustration@gmail.com.

Legal basis and retention

Data is processed on the basis of performing the sales contract and your consent. It is kept for as long as needed to process the order and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

Data sharing

Your data is never sold. It may only be shared with the providers needed to fulfil the order (payment, carrier).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, and to object to it. To exercise these rights, write to dana.illustration@gmail.com.

Cookies

This website uses technical cookies needed for it to work properly (cart, payment).

It also uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, provided by Google, to measure its audience (pages viewed, visit duration, device type, where visitors come from). These tools set audience measurement cookies and send this information to Google, which may process it outside the European Union. This data is used only to understand how the website is used and to improve it.

You can set your browser to refuse cookies, which may limit some features, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on offered by Google.
